Our work

Structures that sit
below sea level

All 16 projects
Loano (SV) Construction of the underwater sections of Loano harbour Our company built Loano harbour, carrying out the seabed levelling and the subsequent laying of the precast concrete blocks, the laying of the mooring chains with their moorings, the demolition of submerged steel and concrete and every kind of underwater work. Pietra Ligure (SV) Construction of the launching ramp at the Rodriquez shipyard Our company built the underwater ramp for launching several ferries carrying cars and passengers within the Iberian Peninsula. We assembled and welded the ramp underwater. Varazze (SV) Construction of the underwater sections of Varazze harbour Our company built Varazze harbour, carrying out the seabed levelling and the subsequent laying of the precast concrete blocks, the demolition of submerged steel and concrete and every kind of underwater work. Arenzano (GE) Construction of the underwater sections of Arenzano harbour Our company built Arenzano harbour, carrying out the seabed levelling and the subsequent laying of the precast concrete blocks, the demolition of submerged steel and concrete and every kind of underwater work. Azimut shipyards Relocation of the underwater system of the Azimut and WS shipyards Removal and reinstallation at another site of the moorings and floating pontoons. Antibes (France) Sun deck construction and anti-jellyfish nets Construction of a sun deck and installation of anti-jellyfish nets in Antibes (France).
