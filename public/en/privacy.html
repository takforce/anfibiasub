Privacy and cookies

How we handle the data of website visitors and customers.

Data controller

Anfibiasub, VAT IT01326660097, Via Dufour 23, Carcare (SV), Italy. Email: termoclino@libero.it.

Public website

The website pages use no contact forms, tracking cookies or analytics. Fonts are hosted on our own server: no data is sent to third parties while you browse. If you contact us by email, phone or WhatsApp, we use your details only to reply and prepare a quote.

Customer area

For customers who receive an account we store: name, email, phone, the boat details entered by the customer, the jobs carried out with their photos and the anode log. We use them to manage maintenance and send the reminders requested. The area uses a single technical cookie, needed to stay signed in. Data is stored on servers in the European Union and is not shared with third parties; service emails are sent through a European provider.

Job photos are visible only to the customer, unless the customer shares them through the dedicated link.

Third-party services

Links to WhatsApp and Google Maps open third-party services with their own privacy policies. On the Tools page the map uses OpenStreetMap and weather data comes from Open-Meteo: the position you mark stays in your browser and is never saved or sent to us.

Your rights

You can ask at any time to access, correct or delete your data by writing to the email address above. You may also lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante).
