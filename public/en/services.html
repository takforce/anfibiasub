Services

What we do
underwater

Ten services, one contact: from cleaning a propeller to building a marina.

Propeller cleaning and polishing

After years of testing we developed a method to clean and descale propellers of any size directly in the water. No haul-out, no yard: costs are halved.

Algae and small shellfish on the underwater hull slow the boat down: you lose speed, burn more fuel and strain the engine. Like a visit to the dentist, regular maintenance costs little and pays for itself straight away in fuel savings.

Our system reduces the diver's effort and does not pollute the harbour water.

Hull cleaning

We clean the hull in the water, choosing the tool for each hull: suitable pads for sailboats, more abrasive pads for motorboats.

For ships we use dedicated variable-trim machines that cover large surfaces quickly.

Anode replacement

Sacrificial anodes protect propellers, shafts and metal parts from galvanic corrosion. Once they are used up, it is your boat's metal that corrodes.

We check, photograph and replace them in the water. In the customer area you find the fitting date and a reminder for the next check.

Underwater civil works

We built the underwater sections of three large marinas. On the seabed we level gravel beds over the foundations placed by the crane.

On top we lay precast reinforced-concrete blocks of 3 x 2 metres with millimetre accuracy: from the bottom we guide the crane operator by underwater telephone, since he cannot see the submerged block.

We pour concrete underwater, both in new harbours and on piers eroded by the sea, and carry out sandbagging and demolition with pneumatic hammers or explosives.

Underwater metalwork

We carry out underwater cutting and welding of every kind, drawing on our experience building launching ramps and cradles.

We specialise in underwater metal cutting and steel fabrication. We build sewer lines and pipework, including laying and flanging.

We also handle maintenance: fitting anodes and zincs, sealing leaks, adding repair sleeves.

Salvage and recovery

We recover objects lost at sea. For smaller items we use parachute lift bags; for heavier ones we work from our pontoon.

With the tug Elansa we also recover boats in difficulty.

Haul-outs and launches

We haul out and launch large boats using a travel lift or other equipment.

We work regularly at the W. Service shipyard in Savona harbour: the diver in the water checks the position of the slings under the hull.

Underwater photo and video

We shoot underwater photos and video in digital format, in the format you prefer, and send them by email.

With a small tethered ROV we inspect the inside and outside of subsea pipes, even at great depth.

Anti-jellyfish nets

We install the Medusa Protect system: a micro-mesh net that keeps jellyfish away and filters foam, oil and floating debris at the surface.

Its stay system keeps the net upright even in strong currents and waves, reducing loads and extending its life. It is designed so that no swimmer can get caught.

It is easy to install, remove, repair and store, and helps beaches earn the Blue Flag.

Design and engineering

For the design of marine and underwater works we team up with independent engineers and surveyors specialised in the field, people we have known through years of joint projects.
