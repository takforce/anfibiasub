    Beaufort scale: wind force and effect on the sea
    ForceDescriptionkm/hKnotsSea state
    0Calm0-1< 1Sea like a mirror.
    1Light air1-51-3Small ripples, no crests.
    2Light breeze6-114-6Small short wavelets, glassy crests that do not break.
    3Gentle breeze12-197-10Large wavelets, crests begin to break, scattered whitecaps.
    4Moderate breeze20-2811-16Longer small waves, frequent whitecaps.
    5Fresh breeze29-3817-21Moderate, longer waves, many whitecaps, some spray.
    6Strong breeze39-4922-27Large waves, extensive white foam crests, some spray.
    7Near gale50-6128-33Sea heaps up, foam from breaking waves blown in streaks along the wind.
    8Gale62-7434-40Moderately high, long waves, crests break into spindrift, well-marked foam streaks.
    9Strong gale75-8841-47High waves, dense foam streaks, crests topple and roll over, spray reduces visibility.
    10Storm89-10248-55Very high waves with long overhanging crests, sea white with foam, reduced visibility.
    11Violent storm103-11756-63Exceptionally high waves, sea covered in foam, visibility seriously reduced.
    12Hurricane≥ 118≥ 64Air filled with foam and spray, sea completely white, visibility nearly zero.
