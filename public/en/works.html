Liguria and French Riviera

Medusa Protect net, protection system against jellyfish

The MEDUSA PROTECT net is an effective system for protecting bathing areas from jellyfish and surface pollutants. It keeps running costs down and makes assembly, installation, removal, repair, transport and storage easier.

Better water quality. The MEDUSA PROTECT net keeps the water clear and free of jellyfish, to the delight of bathers young and old. Its micro-mesh fabric filters out any residue at the surface, such as foam, phenols, oils, minerals, tar residues or floating matter. A surface filtering net is an additional criterion for the «Blue Flag» label.

A system with a high safety margin. The stay system keeps the protective net stable and stops it drifting when currents or waves are strong. It absorbs only a limited part of the force generated, which reduces the loads on it and extends its life. The MEDUSA PROTECT net always stays practically upright despite movement and keeps protecting effectively.

Bather safety. The MEDUSA PROTECT net is designed so that no bather can get caught in it or be hurt by it. It consists of a 20 cm floating cylinder at the surface and a white submerged part that can be seen from a distance.

Full integration and respect for the environment. The submerged part of the MEDUSA PROTECT net is designed so that no fish can get trapped in its mesh.
